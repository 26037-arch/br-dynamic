# Manganese R6/R7 cycle

DKE represents Mn(III) as `MnOH2+` and uses one radical oxidation step and one peroxide
reduction step. The underlying stopped-flow work instead found multiple Mn(III)/peroxide
channels and a stoichiometry of two Mn(III) ions consumed per peroxide under 1.00–3.70 M
perchloric acid and ionic strength 2.23–4.90 M
([Davies, Kirschenbaum, and Kustin, 1968](https://doi.org/10.1021/ic50059a031)).
Those conditions do not match either the historical comparison or the Raman reference.

At the Raman reference’s 0.00667 M total manganese and 0.015 M formal sulfate, sulfate
ion pairing cannot be silently ignored. Plausible species include hexaaqua Mn(II), neutral
MnSO4 ion pairs, hexaaqua Mn(III), hydroxo-Mn(III), and sulfato-Mn(III) complexes. The
available evidence does not uniquely select their distribution immediately after mixing.

**Decision:** the single R6/R7 pair is a Level 0 effective loop. Level 1 remains disabled
until acid activity, sulfate mass balance, and the dominant Mn(II)/Mn(III) coordination states
are defined. Only then can an ET/PCET calculation state meaningful charge, multiplicity,
coordination, and reorganization environments.
