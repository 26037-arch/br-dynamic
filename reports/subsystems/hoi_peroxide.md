# HOI-peroxide reaction: R10

The DKE value is `37 M−1 s−1`. A modern global species-specific analysis obtained
`29 ± 5.2 M−1 s−1` for HOI + H2O2 and a much faster `3.1e8 M−1 s−1` HOI + HO2− channel
([Shin et al., 2020](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.watres.2020.115852)). The numerical agreement
of 37 and 29 is encouraging, but their experimental domains are different: the modern study
used buffers from pH 4 to 12 and used silver below pH 7 to suppress I2 formation.

The acidic Briggs-Rauscher mixture is outside that calibrated pH domain, is unbuffered, and
actively interconverts HOI, I2, and polyiodides. A later mechanistic treatment also reports
buffer catalysis and an IOOH pathway, reinforcing the need to validate the law under BR
conditions rather than transfer one constant blindly.

**Decision:** keep R10 provisionally with the measured 23.8–34.2 M−1 s−1 range as a bounded
condition-transfer screen. Do not classify it as validated until acidic, unbuffered kinetics
with explicit iodine speciation are available.
